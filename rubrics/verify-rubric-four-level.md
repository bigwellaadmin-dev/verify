# VERIFY Rubric (Four-Level)

*For use outside Queensland or in contexts using non A-E reporting language.*

This rubric assesses the VERIFY process, not the quality of the final product. It is designed to be used alongside subject-specific rubrics that assess content knowledge and skill, not instead of them.

**Source:** Sandra Robinson, *Teaching AI to Ask: The VERIFY Framework, Part 4: Assessment and Accountability*. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

| Criterion | Extending | Proficient | Developing | Below |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **V** — Validate the Task | Constructs a precise prompt specification that fully defines task, audience, thinking type, and constraints. Demonstrates sophisticated understanding of how framing shapes output. | Constructs a clear prompt specification addressing task, audience, and thinking type. Identifies the kind of thinking required before opening the AI. | Attempts a prompt specification but it is incomplete or vague. Limited evidence of pre-AI thinking about the task. | No prompt specification evident. Copies task directly into AI. No evidence of task validation. |
| **E** — Examine the Source | Systematically interrogates AI output for accuracy, perspective, and gap. Traces claims to primary sources. Identifies structural biases. | Checks accuracy of key claims. Recognises at least one perspective gap or bias. Verifies at least one claim independently. | Accepts most AI output without interrogation. Makes a surface-level attempt to check one claim. | Accepts AI output without any examination. No attempt to verify claims or identify perspective. |
| **R** — Reflect on Needs and Context | Demonstrates rigorous metacognitive awareness. Clearly articulates how the output fits and does not fit the specific task. Makes targeted revisions. | Reflects on whether the output fits the task and context. Identifies at least one gap and attempts to address it. | Reflection is surface level. May acknowledge the output could be improved without identifying specific gaps. | No evidence of reflection. Accepts AI output as complete without asking whether it fits the task. |
| **I** — Investigate Further | Identifies specific gaps and pursues targeted investigation from multiple independent sources. Findings substantively change or deepen the work. | Identifies at least one significant gap and investigates from an independent source. Findings are integrated meaningfully. | Makes a superficial attempt to investigate further. Adds one source without explaining how it addresses a gap. | No investigation beyond AI output. Accepts what was provided as sufficient. |
| **F** — Filter for Quality and Fairness | Applies the full check question set with precision. Makes multiple targeted changes. Can articulate what was changed and why, including fairness considerations. | Applies at least two check questions and makes at least one substantive change. Identifies a quality or fairness issue and responds to it. | Applies check questions superficially. Makes one minor change or none. Limited ability to identify quality or fairness issues. | No evidence of filtering. Does not apply check questions. Cannot identify quality or fairness issues. |
| **Y** — Your Own Judgement | Demonstrates full intellectual ownership. Defends reasoning fluently under challenge. Work is recognisably the student's own thinking. | Can explain what they decided and why, in their own words, without referring to the work. The judgement is demonstrably their own. | Can summarise the work but struggles to explain the reasoning behind specific decisions. | Cannot explain the reasoning independently of the text. The judgement does not appear to be the student's own. |
